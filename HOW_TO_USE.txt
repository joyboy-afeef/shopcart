==============================================
 SHOPCART - HOW TO USE THIS WEBSITE
 A simple guide to every feature, explained plainly
==============================================

This guide walks you through everything you can do on the site,
both as a shopper and as the site admin. No technical knowledge
needed - just read the section for whatever you're trying to do.


------------------------------------------------
 PART 1: SHOPPING AS A CUSTOMER
------------------------------------------------

1. THE HOME PAGE (the product catalog)
   -----------------------------------
   When you open the site, you land on the home page. This shows
   every product in the store as a grid of cards - each with a
   picture, name, price, and a short description.

2. SEARCHING FOR PRODUCTS
   -----------------------
   Type anything into the search box at the top (like "watch" or
   "backpack") and hit search. The site looks through product
   names and descriptions to find matches - so you don't need to
   know the exact product name, just a related word or two.

3. FILTERING PRODUCTS
   -------------------
   Next to the search box you'll find filter options:
     - Brand: show only products from one brand
     - Rating: show only products with a certain star rating
   You can combine a search with filters to narrow things down
   further.

4. SORTING PRODUCTS
   -----------------
   You can sort the product list by:
     - Price: Low to High
     - Price: High to Low
   Useful if you're on a budget or looking for the priciest option.

5. VIEWING A PRODUCT'S DETAILS
   ----------------------------
   Click any product card to open its detail page. Here you'll see:
     - A bigger picture and full description
     - The price
     - Customer reviews left by other shoppers
     - An average star rating
     - A short AI-written summary of what reviewers are saying
     - Related products you might also like
   From here, you can add the product straight to your cart.

6. THE SHOPPING CART
   ------------------
   Every time you add a product, it goes into your cart (you'll
   see a running total somewhere near the top of the page). In
   the cart you can:
     - Increase or decrease the quantity of an item
     - Remove an item completely
     - See the running total price update automatically

7. LOGGING IN
   ----------
   Before checking out, you'll be asked to log in using your
   phone number (not an email or username). You'll first choose
   your country's calling code from a dropdown, then enter your
   number. This is how the site remembers which orders belong to
   you.

8. CHECKING OUT
   -------------
   Once you're logged in and happy with your cart:
     - Go to Checkout
     - Review everything in your cart one more time
     - Enter your delivery address
     - Confirm the order
   You'll land on a confirmation page showing your order was
   placed successfully.

9. VIEWING YOUR ORDER HISTORY ("My Orders")
   -----------------------------------------
   This page shows every order you've placed, along with its
   current status (e.g. Processing, Shipped, Delivered). Click
   into any order to see its full details.

10. LEAVING FEEDBACK ON AN ORDER
    ------------------------------
    Once you've received an order, you can leave a star rating
    and written feedback on it. This feedback is what shows up
    as "customer reviews" on that product's page for future
    shoppers - so your review actually helps other people decide
    what to buy.

11. THE SUPPORT / HELP CHATBOT
    ----------------------------
    If you have a question about an order, a product, returns,
    or anything else, visit the Support page and type your
    question. An AI chatbot will try to answer it for you based
    on your order history and the store's info.


------------------------------------------------
 PART 2: RUNNING THE STORE AS THE ADMIN
------------------------------------------------

To access any of this, go to the Admin Login page and sign in
with the admin password (set up separately from customer
accounts - see the README file for how the password works).

1. THE ADMIN DASHBOARD
   --------------------
   After logging in, you land on a dashboard showing quick stats:
   how many products you have, how many orders have come in, and
   a breakdown of order statuses (e.g. how many are still
   Processing vs. Delivered).

2. ADDING A NEW PRODUCT
   ---------------------
   Go to "Add Product" and fill in:
     - Product name
     - Description
     - Price
     - Brand
     - An image (upload a picture, or link to one online)
   Once saved, it instantly appears on the storefront for
   customers to see and buy.

3. EDITING OR DELETING A PRODUCT
   ------------------------------
   From "Manage Products," click any product to edit its details
   (name, price, description, image, etc.), or delete it entirely
   if you no longer want to sell it.

4. MANAGING BRANDS
   ----------------
   From "Manage Brands," you can assign or change which brand
   each product belongs to. This is what powers the "Brand"
   filter shoppers see on the home page.

5. MANAGING COUNTRY CALLING CODES
   -------------------------------
   Customers pick their country code when they log in with their
   phone number. From "Manage Codes," you can add new country
   codes or remove ones you don't want to support.

6. MANAGING ORDERS
   ----------------
   From "Manage Orders," you can see every order placed on the
   site. Click into any order to:
     - View exactly what was purchased and by whom
     - Update its status (e.g. mark it as Shipped or Delivered)
     - Edit order details if something needs correcting
     - Delete an order entirely if needed

7. LOGGING OUT
   -----------
   Both customers and admins have their own separate "Logout"
   option, so you can safely sign out of either account type
   when you're done.


------------------------------------------------
 QUICK TIPS
------------------------------------------------
- You don't need an account to browse - only to check out.
- Search + filters + sorting can all be used together.
- Product reviews come directly from customer feedback, so
  encourage buyers to leave a rating - it builds trust for future
  shoppers.
- As admin, anything you add, edit, or delete updates the live
  site immediately - there's no separate "publish" step.
